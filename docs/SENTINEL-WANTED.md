# Milwaukee Sentinel pages still worth saving (2026-11-16)

The Sentinel carried the World's FUN section on FUN's own Sundays from December 1913 to 1915-09-12. Its last
crossword item is that day's solution-only page; Alex found no puzzle after it and none in 1916. So it can't
fill the fall-1915 hole (Nos. 91-100 by the count, 1915-09-19 to 11-14). That needs another paper that carried
FUN then. Those Sundays are recorded as Sentinel no-hits.

**Queued** (batches 18 and 19 in `searches.tsv`): the Sundays where no paper's page is held.

| Sunday | Why |
|---|---|
| 1913-12-21, 1913-12-28 | Nos. 1 and 2. The Sentinel carried FUN by 12-28 (its 01-04 box solves No. 2) |
| 1915-02-14 | the valentine puzzle; closes the break after 02-07 |
| 1915-05-30 | closes the break after 05-23 (the Journal has no 05-30) |

**Not queued.** The queue closes a Sunday as soon as any paper has a copy of it, so these pages can't be
queued. They are still worth saving: each adds a puzzle the record lacks, a solution, or a sharper copy.

| Sunday | What it adds |
|---|---|
| 1914-11-15 | **No. 46 itself**: the Journal never ran it; only its solution is held (Sentinel 11-22) |
| 1914-01-11 | the Sentinel's copy of No. 4, and No. 3's solution |
| 1914-11-01 | the solution to No. 45 (closes that chain break) |
