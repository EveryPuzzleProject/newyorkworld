# Doubts: No. 17 (World 1914-04-12), nyw1914-04-12.xd

1. **3-10 "Used by a cobbler."** The first figure looks more like an 8 than a 3 at 4x. In this face the 3 and 8 are hard to tell apart (the OCR reads 29-30 as "29-80", 31-32 as "81-88" and 23-32 as "28-82"). The grid needs 3-10 (LAST, column 5 from the 3 square; the 8 square is the end of FASTENER). Written 3-10. If it really is an 8, it's a misprint.
2. **19-20 "In hac salus."** The last two words are heavily inked. The letters read best as "hac salus" (the OCR has "hae salma"). The answer is IHS, so "In hac salus" fits. "hoc" is not ruled out for the middle word.
3. **4-11 "X X X X X X X."** Seven X's are printed, with spaces between, then a period. The answer is TENS (X = ten). The spacing is kept as printed.
4. **The ornament square (r7c12).** The square at the right end of the seventh row holds a small cross ornament in both the puzzle and the solution, with no letter. It ends IHS and is the mirror of nothing (r6c12 holds C). Written `#`. It could also be read as part of the decoration and written `_`.
5. **Unclued corners.** The pairs 1-2 (BA, top) and 33-34 (RE, bottom) have no clues; they only serve the downs 1-6, 2-7, 26-33, 27-34. No clue is missing from the list: every other numbered pair is clued.
6. **E-29 and R-30.** The clues start from the printed letters E and R of the EASTER row (r3c4 and r3c9). The xd's Numbering shows them as the letters E, A, S, T, E, R; E-29 means the first E.
7. **8-28 RAPHIS.** The key prints R-A-P-H-I-S in column 10, all clear. RAPHIS (singular of raphides) is as printed.
8. **21-22** runs across the column break: "21-22 Events from which" ends the left column and "time is reckoned." tops the right column. The clue's box is the first part; the continuation has its own entry in "lines".
9. **Title.** From the heading "FUN'S "EASTER" CROSS-WORD PUZZLE", set in title case; "CAN YOU SOLVE IT?" beside the grid is a tagline, not part of the title.
