# Doubts: nyw1914-02-15 (No. 9)

Puzzle: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_02_15_83.png. Solution: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_02_22_83.png ("SOLUTION TO No 9 PUZZLE").
Every square of the solution reads cleanly, and all 30 clue answers run between their end squares letter for letter, with one exception (below).

1. **29-30. The noise a bee makes. ~ BUZZ**: the pair names the wrong end square. BUZZ runs r1c7 (29) down to r4c7, the second Z of the printed PUZZLES, which has no number. The 30 is printed in r10c7, where 30-31 (OPEN) starts. The printed pair is kept. In the Numbering the word ends on the printed Z (r4c7). Perhaps the setter meant "29-Z", following F-4, N-5, P-23 and S-24.
2. **1-2. A barber's implemeut.**: printed "implemeut" (a turned n), clearly so at full zoom. Kept as printed. The answer is RAZOR.
3. **7-15. Assembled,**: printed with a comma, not a period. Kept.
4. **16-25. A small depression make by striking.**: "make" is printed for "made". Kept.
5. **20-27. the first man.**: printed with a lower-case "the". Kept.
6. **6-22**: the OCR read the pair as "8-22", but the scan shows 6-22, and ENTER runs from 6 (r5c11) to 22 (r9c11).
7. Title: "FUN'S Cross-Word Puzzles" comes from the page heading "FUN'S CROSS-WORD PUZZLES" (plural; the heading belongs to the puzzle, not to FUN's masthead). If that counts as FUN's banner, drop the Title.
8. The clue text reads e as c and e as o throughout ("Not carly", "Beforo"). That is the scan's low resolution, not misprints, so those are written as e.
9. The given letters FUN (r2) and PUZZLES (r4) are printed in the grid. In the Numbering they appear as letters, with Z4 used only as BUZZ's end.
