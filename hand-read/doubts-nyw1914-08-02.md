# Doubts: No. 30 (World 1914-08-02), nyw1914-08-02.xd

1. **"2-4 Negative." is a misprint for 3-4.** The clue prints "2-4", clearly. 2 is the end of CAT (1-2); NO runs from 3 to 4 in the grid. The xd and boxes.json give 3-4; boxes.json keeps `"printed": "2-4"`.
2. **r7c0-c2 LOT: blotted in the solution.** The bottom-left three squares of the key are inked over. O and T can be made out, and they agree with DO (13-59) and FIT (11-16). The L can't really be seen. It comes from the clue ("A piece of ground."). r7c0 is not in any down word, so nothing checks it.
3. **r6c16 I (IN, 31-32): smudged in the key.** It looks like "4" or "1". It is I because of IN ("Not out.") and SIP (33-30).
4. **18-21 "Used to catch fish": no period visible.** The line runs to the column edge and the "h" is half-printed. The period may have been lost in printing; I didn't add one.
5. **Printer's letterforms.** In this font e often prints like o ("Negativo", "Favorito", "Poriod", "wipo", "Illuminatod"). I read these as e, the intended letter. The type isn't misspelled; it's just worn.
6. **Clue-number punctuation.** "37 34 Chicken." and "46 49 Possessed." show a space where the hyphen should be. Other clues have a dot ("9·10", "39·40", "56·60"). All are written with a hyphen.
7. **29-30 "You can go no higher.."** There's a second dot after the period. I take it for a speck and wrote one period.
8. **5-6 "Seed·vessel"**: the hyphen is printed raised, like a dot. Written "Seed-vessel."
9. **Intro "it contains".** The line ends at the scan's edge, and the last letter is cut off ("containi"). Read as "contains".
10. **Numbering beyond 35.** The puzzle runs to 60 numbers. gxd's model only goes to a=10..z=35, so 36-60 are written A..Y (header "a=10 z=35 A=36 Y=60"). No given letters in this grid clash with that.
11. **Number placement.** 59 is printed under the middle square of LOT and 60 under the middle square of NOT. They are the lower ends of the two-letter downs 13-59 and 56-60 (DO, DO). Both DOs are confirmed by the key.
