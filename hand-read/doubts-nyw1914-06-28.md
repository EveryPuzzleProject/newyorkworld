# Doubts and oddities: No. 25 (World 1914-06-28), nyw1914-06-28.xd

1. **31-32 "Eagles." gives EARNS.** The solution prints E-A-R-N-S in row 11, every letter clear, and each one is checked by a down word (NICE, TRAP, ERIN, SANE, SUMS). EARNS doesn't answer "Eagles"; ERNES (sea eagles) is surely what the clue means, but it doesn't fit the crossings. Recorded as printed.
2. **Clue numbers with 3 and 8.** In this scan the figures 3 and 8 look almost alike. "8-9" is printed so that it could be read "3-9", and "31-32" and "22-31" look like "81-82" and "22-81". I read them as the grid requires (8-9 BIRD, 31-32 EARNS, 22-31 NICE); there are no squares numbered 81 or 82.
3. **"8-25,"** is printed with a comma after the number pair, not a period. Written "8-25." in the xd's form.
4. **"diers."** (4-5, soldiers): the end mark is speckled and the OCR saw a colon; at 4x it is a period with noise beside it.
5. **"marchers"** (14-15) and **"firecracker"** (8-25): the c and h of "marchers" are damaged (OCR "marobers"); "firecracker" is printed with the fi ligature. Both readings are confident.
6. **Square 20.** The "20" is printed just above the eagle's top star, at the foot of the open column of squares 1-20; it is taken as r4c7, the last letter of GALA. The centre under the eagle (r5c7, r6c6-c8, r7c5-c9, r8c6-c8, r9c7) has no squares in the solution (the "SOLUTION TO No 25 PUZZLE" panel sits there).
7. **Number 36.** The Numbering section runs a-z for 10-35, so 36 is written `A` (`A=36` in its header), as in the run's other files.
8. **Title.** The heading is FUN's usual "FUN'S CROSS WORD PUZZLE" with "JULY 4" added in the same lettering. I took that as the puzzle's own name ("FUN'S July 4 Cross Word Puzzle"); drop the Title if this counts as the banner.
9. **Solution blots.** The far-left S (r7c1, SORT) and the bottom N (r13c7, ERIN) are heavily inked, but the letters are plain and both are confirmed by crossings or the clue.
