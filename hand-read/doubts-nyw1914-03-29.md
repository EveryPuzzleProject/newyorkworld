# Doubts: No. 15 (World 1914-03-29), nyw1914-03-29.xd

1. **Four pairs name the wrong end squares.** All four print clearly (checked at 4x); the xd writes what the grid needs, and boxes.json keeps "printed":
   - "1-1 One of the sons of Noah." is HAM, r2c6-c8, so 1-2.
   - "31-33 Not wet." is DRY, r12c6-c8, so 31-32 (33 is the top square).
   - "1-6 To possess." is HAVE, r2c6-r5c6, so 1-8 (6 is r4c10, not in line with 1). The 6 could be a worn 8, but it looks like a 6.
   - "14-23 Not young." is OLD, r6c12-r8c12, so 14-22 (23 is r9c3).
2. **"7-23 Place of abode."** The e of "Place" is broken ("Plac◦"); read as Place.
3. **"20-29 Wing Shaped."** Capital S as printed.
4. **"27-28 Ecstasy."** Read as printed; the c and s are a little crowded but clear.
5. **Author.** From the contribution credit "sent in by Miss Florence A. Reif of Honesdale, Pa.".
6. **Number.** Printed in the centre panel ("No 15"); the intro also says "the fifteenth in FUN'S series".
7. **Title omitted.** Only FUN's banner "FUN'S CROSS-WORD PUZZLES / Can You Solve Them?" is printed.
8. **Centre panel.** The diamond-shaped centre (r5c7; r6c6-8; r7c5-9; r8c6-8; r9c7) is `_`; the solution prints "SOLUTION TO CROSS-WORD PUZZLE No 15" across it. A speck in r5c7 of the solution is not a letter.
9. **Clue order.** Two printed columns, left then right; 27-28 to 35-36 head the right column and are kept there.
