# Doubts: No. 1 (World 1913-12-21), nyw1913-12-21.xd

Puzzle: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1913_12_21_68.png. Solution: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1913_12_28_78.png, printed upside down (the boxes file gives the key's corners in the puzzle's orientation).

## Readings and oddities
1. **No clue for 2-3 (SALES).** The Sentinel's left column starts at "4-5. A written acknowledgment." Nothing is printed for 2-3. The xd has 30 clues and none for SALES. gxd has "2-3. What bargain hunters enjoy." (from another source, presumably the World).
2. **"38-39. What this puzzle is."** Printed clearly as 38-39 (checked at 5x). The grid has no 38 or 39; the answer HARD runs 18-19. Kept as printed.
3. **"10-12. The fibre of the gomuti palm."** The answer DOH runs 10-18 (column 2). The 12 is clear. Kept as printed.
4. **"26-30. A river in Russia."** The answer NEVA runs 23-30 (column 6). The 26 is clear. Kept as printed.
5. **"22-23. An animal of prey."** The first digit of 22 is a little blurred and could be taken for a 3; it matches the second 2 in shape, so read 22 (the grid agrees).
6. **"9-25. To sink in mud."** The answer MIRED is a past participle; the clue reads "To sink", not "Sunk". Printed clearly; kept.
7. **Damaged letters read through.** "To clude." is read "To elude." (broken e); "Russin.'" is "Russia." with a stray mark after it; "Onc." is "One."; "A boy" OCR "hoy". Each is plain in the print once zoomed.
8. **Line-break hyphens joined.** "acknowl-edgment", "go-muti", "sea-shore". The last could be a printed compound "sea-shore"; it falls at a line end, so it is written "seashore" (as gxd has it).
9. **"24-31. To agree with ."** A space before the period, a printer's spacing quirk; written "To agree with."
10. **Title.** The Sentinel's heading reads "FUN'S CROSS-WORD PUZZLE." (clear at 2x). The xd uses it in title case, as the model does for all-caps headings.

## Differences from gxd's nyworld/1913/nyw1913-12-21.xd
- **Title.** gxd: "FUN'S Word-Cross Puzzle." The Sentinel: "FUN'S CROSS-WORD PUZZLE." The solution's caption calls it "the word cross puzzle".
- **Author.** gxd: Arthur Wynne. The Sentinel prints no credit; the xd has no Author.
- **Notes.** gxd's Notes are the instructions only; the instructions' text is the same.
- **Grid.** Identical, letter for letter (the Sentinel's solution confirms every square).
- **Clue 2-3.** gxd has "2-3. What bargain hunters enjoy. ~ SALES"; the Sentinel prints no such clue.
- **Clue numbers.** gxd 18-19, 10-18, 23-30; the Sentinel prints 38-39, 10-12, 26-30 (items 2-4).
- **Clue text.** gxd "9-25. Sunk in mud."; the Sentinel "9-25. To sink in mud." All other clue wording and punctuation match gxd, and so does the order.
- **Numbering section.** gxd's Numbering has no 32 (r4c7) or 33 (r10c7), and labels row 10 q r s (26 27 28), row 11 t u, row 12 v w... with x=34. The Sentinel prints 26, 33, 27 in row 10 (c4, c7, c10), 28 and 29 in row 11, 30 and 31 in row 12, 32 at r4c7 and 34 at r13c7. The xd maps every printed number (a=10 ... y=34, sequential).
