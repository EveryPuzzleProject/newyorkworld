# Doubts: nyw1914-02-22 (No. 10, the George Washington puzzle)

Puzzle: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_02_22_80.png. Solution: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_03_01_85.png (no heading in the solution box; the caption beside it says "Herewith is shown the solution to the George Washington cross-word puzzle").
The solution reads cleanly in every square, and all 40 answers run between their end squares letter for letter.

1. **24-25. A black bird. ~ OSEL**: the key prints OSEL, a rare spelling of ousel/ouzel. The scan is clear, so it is kept.
2. **6-17. A rodent. ~ HARE**: the key's answer, though a hare is not a rodent. Kept.
3. **12-13. To roll a flag around a staff,**: printed with a comma (or a period with a speck), not a clean period. Kept as a comma.
4. **36-37**: the hyphen between 36 and 37 is not visible in the scan ("36 37"). There is also a stray mark after "George" in that clue, a speck and not a comma.
5. **6-7**: a stray printed rule crosses the second line ("chopped down the cherry"). The words under it are legible.
6. Damaged type, read as the words they plainly are: 8-32 "Soldiers" (the ie is broken), 12-28 "George" and "in" (both broken), 3-10 "reflected" (one e broken), 40-41 (the 4 of 41 is broken).
7. The scan reads e as o throughout (Dolaware, Revore, plato). These are written as e, not kept as misprints.
8. **7-18**: the OCR missed the first line ("7-18 What Washington caught"). Its box is drawn by eye and also cites o104 and o105.
9. Intro (a line, not a clue), transcribed as printed: "occasio:" (a broken n), "Georga" (for George), and "puzzles" at the end with no period.
10. Title "A George Washington Cross-Word Puzzle" is the heading printed over this puzzle. There is no puzzle credit; the drawings are signed CALLAHAN (recorded as an art credit line, not as Author).
11. The Number (10) comes from the intro only. The centre of the grid holds the portrait, not a number.
