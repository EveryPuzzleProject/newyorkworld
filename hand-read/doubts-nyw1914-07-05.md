# Doubts and oddities: No. 26 (World 1914-07-05), nyw1914-07-05.xd

1. **27-34 printed twice.** The list prints "26-33. Destiny." then "27-34. Destiny." then "27-34. A mountain." The grid's 27-34 is BERG (column 8), which fits "A mountain"; "27-34. Destiny." looks like a repeated line from the typesetting. Both are kept in the xd, as printed, each with BERG; drop the "Destiny." one if a misprinted duplicate shouldn't stand as a clue.
2. **18-19 "A small spike."** The line is badly printed (broken type: it looks like "ʌ smaıı spike."). The reading is certain from the sense and the answer NAIL.
3. **Clue order.** 21-22 is printed before 18-19 in the first column; kept as printed.
4. **"2- 3." and "2- 9."** etc. are printed with a space after the hyphen (also "4- 5.", "6- 7.", "8- 9."); written "2-3." in the xd's form.
5. **"coresponding"** (one r) in the intro is the printer's spelling; kept in Notes.
6. **Author.** From the intro, "contributed by Charles L. Rives of Spring Valley, N. Y."; not a byline under the grid.
7. **Solution centre.** The key's panel reads "SOLUTION TO X-WORD PUZZLE No 26"; the "26" is small and blurred (it could pass for "20"), but No. 26 is the only puzzle that fits the grid.
8. **Number 36.** Written `A` in the Numbering section (`A=36` in its header), since the letters stop at z=35.
