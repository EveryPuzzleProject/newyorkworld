# Doubts: nyw1914-03-08 (No. 12)

Puzzle: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_03_08_82.png. Solution: Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_03_15_86.png ("SOLUTION TO No 12 PUZZLE"; the page's other grid is the "65" magic square, not this puzzle).
All 32 answers run between their end squares letter for letter with the printed key. Every square of the key reads cleanly; a printed rule crosses the SETTLER row, but the letters are legible.

## Doubts and oddities

1. **Credit**: "Today's cross work puzzle, the twelfth in FUN'S series, was sent in by Miss Evangeline Kearney of 207 Winthrop street, Brooklyn." This gives Author: Evangeline Kearney. "cross work" (for "cross word") is printed so and kept in the line. The comma after "series" is a small hook at the line's end; I read it as a comma, but it could be a speck.
2. Damaged numbers in the grid: the top square's 33 prints like "13" or "43", and the bottom square's 36 prints like "96". The clues 33-34 and 35-36, and the key, fix them as 33 and 36.
3. **11-19. A chart.**: there is a smudge after "chart"; read as the period.
4. **35-36. A color. ~ GRAY**, **24-31. A small insect. ~ GNAT**: plain. The scan reads e as c in "inseet" and "Fiesh"; written as e.
5. The intro says "the solutions of last week's puzzle" (plural); the solution caption says "Today's cross work puzzle is more difficult than last week's". Both kept as printed in the lines.

## Differences from the shipped file (newyorkworld/xd/nyw1914-03-08.xd, from the Minneapolis Journal)

- **Grid**: identical.
- **Numbering**: the same positions. The legend reads "a=10 z=35 A=36" here and "a=10 A=36" there.
- **Clue text**: identical, all 32 clues.
- **Clue order**: the shipped file puts 33-34 at the head of the downs and 35-36 at the end, and sorts the downs by number. The Sentinel prints 1-2 … 29-30 in the left column, then 31-32, 33-34, 35-36, 1-8, 3-12, 5-27, 7-23, 11-19, 2-9, 4-13, 6-28, 14-22, 10-26, 21-30, 25-32, 20-29, 24-31 in the right. This file follows the printed order. (The Sentinel's order of the downs differs from No. 11's.)
- **Author**: the Sentinel prints the Kearney credit (item 1). The shipped file has no Author, and its "Printed with puzzle" text shows the Journal's own intro ("TODAY'S cross-word puzzle is the twelfth in the series...") without the credit, plus "(Copyright, 1914, by the Du Pay Syndicate.)". So the Journal seems to have dropped the reader's credit. Worth adding the Author to the shipped file.
- **Headers**: this file has Title (FUN'S Cross-Word Puzzles), Author, Copyright, Number and Notes. The shipped file has no Title, Author, Copyright or Notes, and carries Rights, Solution, Solution-Source, Source, Assembly, Numbering-Rule, Clues, Text-Review and Number. Both give Number 12.
- **Extras**: the shipped file's "Printed with puzzle" OCR block, review flags and "Text step, unsure" notes are not in this file.
