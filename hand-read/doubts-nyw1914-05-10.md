# Doubts: No. 21 (World 1914-05-10), nyw1914-05-10.xd

Puzzle page Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_05_10_89.png; solution Milwaukee_Sentinel_1914_05_17_89.png. The key is sharp and every one of the 52 answers runs between its printed end squares, letter for letter with the key.

1. **38-49 "A wooden drinking vessel." gives RABIT.** The key prints R-A-B-I-T down column 8 (EIDER, CACAO, RUBRICATE, RIGLETS, TAINT), every letter clear. RABIT is not a word I know for a drinking vessel. It is recorded as the key prints it.
2. **8-23 "To surpass." gives REVIE**, from SEPTEMBER, SOWER, REVEL, RISES and EIGHT. "Revie" is an old card-play word (to outbid), so the answer is probably intended, but it is obscure.
3. **20-36 "Shyly." gives SHILY** (a variant spelling). **39-50 "German for dwelling place." gives STATT.** Both are recorded as printed.
4. **Printed pair numbers that look wrong because of the type.** In this face a 3 looks like an 8. The page appears to read "18-37", "31-82", "33-34" (as "88-34"), "3ō-48", "89-50" and "48-52". Read with that in mind, and checked against the grid, they are 13-37, 31-32, 33-34, 35-48, 39-50 and 43-52. The xd uses those numbers. 18-37 can't be right: 18 is the end of SALTS, and 18 to 37 isn't a line.
5. **"1733 Qpality affecting the sense of taste."** The hyphen is missing ("1733"). The xd writes 17-33. "Qpality" is a misprint for Quality; it is kept.
6. **Right-edge letters.** The right column of clues runs into the page's black edge. The last letter or two of "color.", "next", "sense", "vessel.", "house." and "place." are clipped or blotted, and are read from what's left plus the sense.
7. **Damaged letters read from context.** "To tint wit[h] red." (the h is broken). "What your heart does." (the word "does." is blotted). "31-46 A fright." (the A has a stroke through it, an ink flaw). "Before 9 o'clock." (the o carries a stray mark, "ō'clock").
8. **Credit name.** The intro reads "Miss Elizabeth M. Rutzinger" with the R faint; it could be read as B ("Butzinger"). The xd has Rutzinger.
9. **Grid numbers.** Most of the printed end-square numbers are blotted (53, 54, 10 to 20, 38 to 52 especially). Each is placed by its position (numbers run across the rows, end squares at the ends of each run) and confirmed by all 52 answers fitting. 53 is in the single top square and 54 is in r5c10, just above the hole. 55 is in r15c10, just below the hole, and 56 is in the bottom square.
10. **Numbering above 35.** The model's letter scheme stops at z=35. This puzzle runs to 56, so the Numbering section carries on with capitals, A=36 to U=56, as its header says. There are no given letters in this puzzle, so the capitals can't be mistaken for them.
11. **Title.** It is taken from the heading, "FUN'S CROSS-WORD PUZZLE No. 21", as the earlier xds do. Drop it if the banner counts as FUN's and not the puzzle's own name.
12. **"A genii worshiped in Burma."** "genii" is kept as printed.
