# Doubts and oddities: No. 29 (World 1914-07-26), nyw1914-07-26.xd

1. **Layout in one grid.** The five pieces are placed on one 17 x 20 lattice. The left band takes c1-c8, the right band c13-c20, and the 4x4 c9-c12, which matches the page. On the page, though, the two 3x3 squares sit half a square off the lattice: their columns run from c9.5 to c12.5, and they are written at c10-c12. The 4x4 sits half a row off too: its rows run from r7.5 to r11.5, and it is written at r8-r11. So in the overlay their printed rules fall halfway between lattice lines; this is expected. No piece touches another.
2. **Squares 71 and 72 are not numbered in the puzzle.** The list has "71-42 Dexterity." (ART) and "72-40 Within." (IN). These start in the right band's top row, at r1c18 and r1c19. Those two squares are printed blank (checked at 3x). The xd numbers them 71 and 72 as the list implies.
3. **3 and 8 look alike in this bold face.** Several pairs read one way but the grid needs the other. "48-47 A grassy plain." is 43-47 (LEA). "89-51 Tangible." is 39-51 (TACTILE). "78-75 An instrument for piercing holes." is 73-75 (AWL). The same holds for many others ("88-34", "87-38", "89-40", "48-44", "58-54" and more), which read as 33-34, 37-38, 39-40, 43-44, 53-54. All were read as the grid requires. Only the three named first would otherwise point at real but wrong squares.
4. **77-79 is printed twice**, both times "The organ of vision." (EYE). Both are kept, as printed. The across 73-75 (AWL) is the clue printed last ("78-75"), not in sequence with the 3x3's other clues.
5. **Smudged clue text.** The bottom of the first column and the end of the third column are heavily inked: "55-56 To corrode.", "53-54 To produce figures on a copper plate.", "41-49 To analyze.", "94-98 A unit.", "95-99 Nine and one.", "96-100 Water in a solid mass." and "73-75 ... holes.". I read the words from their shapes and from their answers (FRET, ETCH, PARSE, ACE, TEN, ICE, AWL). "unit" and "mass" are the least legible; "A unit." for ACE is a confident fit.
6. **Answers as printed.** These are all clear in the solution. WROT is clued "Simplified for wrought.". WEELS (15-23, "Whirlpools."), OLITORY (22-34) and OTARY (24-32) are rare words. ETNA (51-52) is "A table cooking utensil.", the spirit-lamp sense. ASTERIA (48-60) is "A variety of sapphire.".
7. **No number, title or heading.** The page prints no "FUN'S CROSS WORD PUZZLE" banner and no number for this puzzle; No. 29 is from puzzles.tsv. Number is left out of the headers, and the Author comes from the intro credit ("from Aloysius E. Pracny of Jackson street, Brooklyn").
8. **Numbers above 61.** The Numbering section uses a-z for 10-35 and A-Z for 36-61. It then uses Greek α-ω for 62-86 (U+03B1-U+03C9 in order, so ς is 79) and Cyrillic б-т for 87-104 (U+0431-U+0442 in order), as its header's anchors declare. Some Cyrillic letters (е, о, р, с) look like Latin ones but are different characters.
9. **Clue punctuation.** The clues are printed "1-4 A narrow passage." with no period after the pair; they are written "1-4." in the xd's form.
