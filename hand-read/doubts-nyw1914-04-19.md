# Doubts: No. 18 (World 1914-04-19), nyw1914-04-19.xd

1. **Diagonal words.** 1-3 GAMMA, 4-2 SNARE, 7-5 FRETS and 6-8 SITES run diagonally, from the corner squares to the cross in the centre, as the intro says. xd has no diagonal direction, so these four pairs name diagonal squares; a tool that only knows across and down will not find them. 4-2 reads from the 4 square (r5c7) up to the 2 square (r1c11); 7-5 reads from the bottom-left corner up to r7c5.
2. **Unclued word.** Column 7 below the centre, r7c7 to r10c7 (from the 6 square to the 22 square), reads S-R-E-M in the key. It is not a word and has no clue; its letters belong to STIR, STEARIN, ESTREAT and AIM. The matching column 5 (5-21 SETA) is clued. Nothing is missing from the clue list.
3. **Clue spelling kept.** The intro prints "addtiion" (for "addition"); kept. 17-18 prints "liquor" with a broken first i (OCR "lquor"); read as "liquor". 25-26 "Austria." is heavily inked but clear in context.
4. **18-4 letter-spaced.** "T h e y c o m e b e f o r e Christmas." is set letter-spaced to fill the line. Written "They come before Christmas."
5. **Number 32's position.** The printed 32 sits at the bottom of row 9, straddling the rule between columns 7 and 8. INERTIA (31-32) needs it in column 8, which is where most of it sits.
6. **Number and Title.** No number is printed in the grid; the intro's "the eighteenth in FUN'S series" gives 18. The Title comes from the heading "FUN'S CROSS-WORD PUZZLES" (plural as printed). "Can You Solve Them?" is a tagline.
7. **Author.** From the intro: "was sent in by Alex B. Miller of Portsmouth, Va." It's a contribution credit, not a byline.
8. **Numbering past z.** There are 36 numbers; 36 is written `A` (a=10, z=35, A=36), as nyw1914-08-02.xd does.
9. **Lattice.** The puzzle grid is printed slightly skewed (the bottom edge sits a few pixels left of the top edge); the corners follow it.
