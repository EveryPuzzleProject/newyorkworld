**No answer the owner can give on the proof page ships any of the six on merged code.** Three things
in shared code stand in front of all 22 puzzles, before any puzzle's own problems:

1. **The ship check doesn't read the World's xd.** It counts only clue lines labelled `A1.` / `D1.` and
   only `#` as a block, so every puzzle whose clues are labelled by their pairs (`1-2.`) and whose
   template squares are `_` is found to have "slots the grid numbers with no clue" (`clue-missing`) and
   odd squares. `clue-missing` is one of the findings no Ready lifts. Checked by simulation (a copy of

   `ship.py` line 388). Every Ready given so far carries edits: the owner's on Nos. 7 and 8, the AI's on
   Nos. 11 and 12. The other road for an edit, the re-place loop of 2026-11-05, takes the square
   numbers only: the owner's clue, key-square, grid-shape and printed-line fixes on Nos. 7, 8 and 9

   the proof page asks for them all and gets the first 1,000 by id (the database's row cap), which now
   end at judge1927-04-16. A link to any puzzle after that falls through to the first open puzzle:
   `?p=nyw1914-03-08-L000` opens collegian1976-03-11-L001, with no message. This began with the
